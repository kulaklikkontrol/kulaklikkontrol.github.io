Huawei FreeBuds on a Mac: Pro 5 only, read only

Huawei earbud settings live on the phone side, and options for Mac users are thin. That’s why the Huawei adapter in Headphone Control starts cautiously: FreeBuds Pro 5 only, and read only.

Experimental: we haven’t tested this model on our own hardware

In Headphone Control, only the WH-1000XM5 has been tested on real hardware. No Huawei model, the FreeBuds Pro 5 included, has been tried on our own hardware.

Everything on this page comes from code and documentation; how it behaves on your headset is what your report will show. If nothing answers, the basics (connection, playback, macOS battery) keep running.

What a FreeBuds Pro 5 doesn’t show on a Mac

On a Mac the FreeBuds Pro 5 plays audio, but the Mac can’t see the ANC mode or the battery the earbuds themselves report.

The adapter opens a single serial control session (SPP) and writes the battery and ANC replies to the diagnostic log. The battery percentage on screen is the macOS value in this build. If the phone app is connected at the same time, replies may not come.

Nothing is written: you can’t change ANC from the Mac in this build. The goal is to confirm the protocol from real user logs, since we don’t own a Pro 5.

Which FreeBuds?

Only the HUAWEI FreeBuds Pro 5 is matched. On other models such as FreeBuds Pro 4, FreeBuds 6i or FreeClip the adapter stays out of the way; audio, microphone and universal sound handoff keep running through macOS.

How to turn it on

  1. Install the TestFlight beta; the Huawei adapter ships in 1.5 (14).
  2. Connect the FreeBuds Pro 5 in System Settings → Bluetooth.
  3. In Settings → Brands → Experimental brand adapters turn on the Huawei switch. It is off by default, and in 1.5 (14) the label may read in Turkish: “Huawei FreeBuds Pro 5 verisini oku (deneysel)”.
  4. Reconnect the earbuds.

Questions

What about FreeBuds Pro 4 or FreeBuds 6i?

They are not matched. On a Mac you keep what macOS offers (audio, mic, battery if reported) plus the universal sound handoff.

Why can’t I change ANC from the Mac?

This build is read-only on purpose. Write commands won’t be added before the read replies are confirmed on a real Pro 5.

Is the log sent anywhere?

The app asks for no account. Sharing the log is up to you: copy the device report and send it by email or through GitHub.
