What is missing for Sony headphones on a Mac?

macOS treats a Sony headset as a plain Bluetooth audio device: playback, volume, microphone. Switching between ANC and ambient sound, picking an EQ curve or seeing the headset’s own battery percentage means reaching for your phone, which is awkward in the middle of a call.

On the WH-1000XM5 that gap is closed, and we confirmed it on our own WH-1000XM5. Read from the Mac: ANC/ambient mode, EQ, volume, battery and connection state. Changed from the Mac: only ANC/ambient, EQ and volume; battery and connection state are read-only. Other Sony models appear to use the same control family, but none of them has been on our desk; user reports will decide their status.

2-minute test

About two minutes from install to report. “Nothing answered” is a useful result too: it is how we learn which models don’t respond.

  1. Install the TestFlight beta on your Mac (link below).
  2. Connect the headphones first in System Settings → Bluetooth.
  3. Sony has no switch under Settings → Experimental brand adapters; that section is for other brands. Sony control lives in Settings → Brands → Sony → “Sony control adapter” and is on by default.
  4. If you turned it off and on again, reconnect the headphones: adapter settings apply on reconnect.
  5. Copy the diagnostic report: in 1.5 (14) open the main window’s Device → “Report my device”; in 1.6 it is Settings → Diagnostics → Copy report. The report contains no Bluetooth name, address or serial number.
  6. Open the TestFlight app, pick Headphone Control, choose Send Beta Feedback and paste the report. These are the reports the verification dashboard counts.

Questions

Is there an official Mac app for Sony headphones?

Sony’s official headphone app runs on iPhone and Android; its App Store listing (checked 26 November 2026) offers no Mac version. On a Mac a Sony headset plays audio, but noise cancelling, ambient sound and EQ stay on your phone.

Can a WH-1000XM4 or WH-1000XM6 be controlled from a Mac?

Experimental. We have not tested these models on our own hardware. The app shows the controls the headset answers; if it doesn’t answer, audio, connection and macOS battery keep going. Send your result and the status changes on real evidence.

What is the difference between the App Store version and the TestFlight beta?

Version 1.0 on the App Store targets the Sony WH-1000X series. The 17-model Sony list and the universal sound handoff come with the 1.5 beta via TestFlight.

Is Sony behind this app?

No. Headphone Control is an independent developer project; we have no affiliation with Sony.

Does the number of reports change the status label?

No. The verification dashboard shows the number of successful TestFlight reports next to each model’s label. The count never replaces the label, and no fixed number turns a model into “verified”.
