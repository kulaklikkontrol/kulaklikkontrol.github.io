Sony headphones on a Mac: what is verified, what is experimental

A Sony headset pairs with a Mac and plays audio, but noise cancelling, ambient sound and EQ stay in the phone app. Headphone Control brings those settings to the Mac menu bar. In the Sony line-up exactly one model has been verified on real hardware: the WH-1000XM5.

In Headphone Control, only the WH-1000XM5 has been tested on real hardware. This applies to the 16 Sony models other than the WH-1000XM5. On them the app starts on the macOS layer and only shows the controls the headphones answer.

Everything on this page comes from code and documentation; how it behaves on your headset is what your report will show. If nothing answers, the basics (connection, playback, macOS battery) keep running.

What is missing for Sony headphones on a Mac?

macOS treats a Sony headset as a plain Bluetooth audio device: playback, volume, microphone. Switching between ANC and ambient sound, picking an EQ curve or seeing the headset’s own battery percentage means reaching for your phone, which is awkward in the middle of a call.

On the WH-1000XM5 that gap is closed: ANC/ambient, EQ, volume, battery and connection state were read and changed from the Mac, and this was confirmed on our own WH-1000XM5. Other Sony models appear to use the same control family, but none of them has been on our desk; user reports will decide their status.

How to turn it on

  1. Install the TestFlight beta on your Mac (link below).
  2. Connect the headphones first in System Settings → Bluetooth.
  3. Sony has no switch under Settings → Experimental brand adapters; that section is for other brands. Sony control lives in Settings → Brands → Sony → “Sony control adapter” and is on by default.
  4. If you turned it off and on again, reconnect the headphones: adapter settings apply on reconnect.

Experimental. We have not tested these models on our own hardware. The app shows the controls the headset answers; if it doesn’t answer, audio, connection and macOS battery keep going. Send your result and the status changes on real evidence.
