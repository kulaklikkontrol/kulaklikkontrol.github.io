Technics EAH-AZ100 on a Mac: seeing multipoint and case battery

The EAH-AZ100 can stay connected to more than one device at a time, but from the Mac you can’t see which devices those are or how much charge is left in the case. The experimental Technics adapter tries to read them and changes no settings.

Where the Mac is blind with an AZ100

To a Mac the AZ100 is an audio device. The noise cancelling / ambient setting and the multipoint list live in the phone app.

The adapter goes through a model gate first: if the earbuds don’t identify as EAH-AZ100, nothing is read. If they do, the ANC/ambient mode, multipoint state, left and right earbud battery and case battery are read.

Two commands are never sent, on purpose: factory reset and the LE Audio switch. One wipes pairings, the other changes the connection type; neither belongs in an experimental adapter.

Which Technics models?

Only the EAH-AZ100. Other Technics models such as EAH-AZ80, EAH-AZ60M2 or EAH-A800 don’t pass the model gate; on a Mac they keep macOS audio and the universal sound handoff.

How to turn it on

  1. Install the TestFlight beta; the Technics adapter ships in 1.5 (14).
  2. Connect the AZ100 in System Settings → Bluetooth.
  3. In Settings → Brands → Experimental brand adapters turn on the Technics switch. It is off by default; in 1.5 (14) the label may read in Turkish: “Technics verisini oku (deneysel)”.
  4. Reconnect the earbuds.

Questions

Can I change ANC on the AZ100 from the Mac?

Not in this build. The adapter is read-only; it only reads the mode.

What about the EAH-AZ80?

The adapter recognises only the EAH-AZ100. On an AZ80 you keep universal sound handoff and the battery macOS shows.

Can the adapter reset my earbuds?

No. There is no reset command in the adapter, and the LE Audio switch is never sent either.
