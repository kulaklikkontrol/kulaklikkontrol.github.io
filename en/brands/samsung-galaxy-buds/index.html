Samsung Galaxy Buds on a Mac: battery for the older generation

Connect Galaxy Buds to a Mac and audio plays, but for left, right and case battery you look at your phone. Samsung’s earbud app is a phone app. The experimental Samsung adapter tries to bring that battery reading to the Mac for older Buds.

What Galaxy Buds are missing on a Mac

Older Buds (Galaxy Buds 2019, Buds+, Buds Live, Buds Pro) send status messages on their own while connected. The adapter listens to them: earbud and case battery, and whether the buds are in your ears.

The adapter writes nothing. ANC, ambient sound, EQ and touch settings don’t change from the Mac, and the ANC state on Buds Live and Buds Pro isn’t shown in this build.

Galaxy Buds2, Buds2 Pro, Buds FE, Buds3 and later are not matched; the Mac side is planned. Those models only keep the experimental universal layer, which hasn’t been tried on any Buds model either.

Which Galaxy Buds?

Four models are matched: Galaxy Buds (2019), Buds+, Buds Live, Buds Pro. From the Buds2 generation on, the message layout differs, so those are out of scope in this build.

How to turn it on

  1. Install the TestFlight beta; the Samsung adapter ships in 1.5 (14).
  2. Connect the Buds in System Settings → Bluetooth.
  3. In Settings → Brands → Experimental brand adapters turn on the Samsung switch. It is off by default; in 1.5 (14) the label may read in Turkish: “Samsung Galaxy Buds pilini oku (deneysel)”.
  4. Reconnect the Buds and take them out of the case; battery messages arrive whenever the buds send them.

Questions

Will Buds3 Pro show battery on a Mac?

The adapter doesn’t recognise the Buds3 Pro. If the earbuds report battery to macOS, the macOS value shows.

Does the adapter write anything to the earbuds?

No. It only listens and doesn’t touch the earbuds’ settings. If nothing answers, the basics keep running.

Can I change ANC on Buds Pro from the Mac?

Not in this build; the adapter is limited to battery and in-ear state.
