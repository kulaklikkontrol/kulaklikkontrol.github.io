In Headphone Control, only the WH-1000XM5 has been tested on real hardware. We have no MOMENTUM 4. The adapter follows MIT-licensed protocol notes and is unit-tested against packet captures from a real headset that those notes publish.

What a Mac can’t see on a MOMENTUM 4

The MOMENTUM 4 plays audio on a Mac like any Bluetooth headset, and macOS may show its battery. Noise cancelling, transparency, bass boost and the EQ only live in the official phone app (iPhone and Android).

The experimental Sennheiser adapter speaks the headset’s control protocol over the serial control channel. In 1.5 (14) it only reads: battery, model, firmware, ANC state, noise modes and bass boost. It changes nothing.

Build 1.6, now in Apple’s beta review, adds changes: ANC, transparency, adaptive ANC, wind reduction, bass boost and a 5-band EQ. Every write passes an allow-list and is read back; a request with no answer in 3 seconds is skipped.

Which Sennheiser models?

The adapter targets the MOMENTUM 4 Wireless. Other Sennheiser models are not targeted and untested; if a headset doesn’t answer, the Sennheiser controls never appear and the universal layer keeps running.

2-minute test

About two minutes from install to report. “Nothing answered” is a useful result too: it is how we learn which models don’t respond.

  1. Install the TestFlight beta. The Sennheiser adapter ships in 1.5 (14), which the public link now installs.
  2. Close the official phone app on your iPhone, or turn off Bluetooth there for a moment: one control session at a time.
  3. Connect the MOMENTUM 4 in System Settings → Bluetooth.
  4. In Settings → Brands → Experimental brand adapters turn on “Read Sennheiser data (experimental)”. In 1.5 (14) the label may read in Turkish: “Sennheiser verisini oku (deneysel)”. In 1.6 it becomes “Read and control Sennheiser data”.
  5. Reconnect the headset; adapter settings apply on reconnect.
  6. Copy the diagnostic report: in 1.5 (14) open the main window’s Device → “Report my device”; in 1.6 it is Settings → Diagnostics → Copy report. The report contains no Bluetooth name, address or serial number.
  7. Open the TestFlight app, pick Headphone Control, choose Send Beta Feedback and paste the report. These are the reports the verification dashboard counts.

Questions

Is there an official Mac app for Sennheiser?

Sennheiser’s official app runs on iPhone and Android; its App Store listing (checked 26 November 2026) offers no Mac version. On a Mac the MOMENTUM 4 plays audio, but noise cancelling, transparency, bass boost and EQ stay on your phone.

Do I need a Sennheiser account?

No. The app asks for no account and sends nothing to a server.

Can I change the EQ from the Mac?

Not in 1.5 (14), which only reads. Build 1.6, in beta review, adds a 5-band EQ and the ANC, transparency, adaptive ANC, wind reduction and bass boost controls, each with an allow-list and read-back.

Nothing shows up. What now?

Close the official phone app, toggle the switch, reconnect the headset and look again. If it still stays empty, copy the report and send it through TestFlight: “no answer” is useful.

Does the number of reports change the status label?

No. The verification dashboard shows the number of successful TestFlight reports next to each model’s label. The count never replaces the label, and no fixed number turns a model into “verified”.
