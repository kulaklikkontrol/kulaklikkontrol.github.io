What a Nothing Ear is missing on a Mac

On the Ear series you pinch the stem or open your phone to move between noise cancelling and transparency. Left, right and case battery are a phone-only view too.

The adapter first runs an activation handshake with the earbuds. If that holds, battery and ANC state are read, and it then tries to set the ANC mode and an EQ preset. If the handshake gets no answer, the Nothing controls don’t appear.

CMF Buds and CMF Headphone models are not matched in this build; Mac control is planned. With a CMF product only the experimental universal layer kicks in today, and it hasn’t been tried on any CMF model.

The adapter recognises six models: Ear (1), Ear (2), Ear (2024), Ear (a), Ear (3), Headphone (1). No CMF product is on that list. If it grows, this page changes, and user reports will drive that.

2-minute test

About two minutes from install to report. “Nothing answered” is a useful result too: it is how we learn which models don’t respond.

  1. Install the TestFlight beta. The Nothing adapter ships in 1.5 (14), which the public link now installs.
  2. Connect the earbuds in System Settings → Bluetooth.
  3. Open the Settings → Brands → Nothing row and turn on the Nothing switch. In 1.5 (14) the label may still read in Turkish: “Nothing verisini oku ve kontrol et”. (The other experimental brands sit in Settings → Experimental brand adapters.)
  4. Reconnect the earbuds.
  5. Copy the diagnostic report: in 1.5 (14) open the main window’s Device → “Report my device”; in 1.6 it is Settings → Diagnostics → Copy report. The report contains no Bluetooth name, address or serial number.
  6. Open the TestFlight app, pick Headphone Control, choose Send Beta Feedback and paste the report. These are the reports the verification dashboard counts.

Questions

Is there an official Mac app for Nothing / CMF?

Nothing’s official app runs on iPhone and Android; its App Store listing (checked 26 November 2026) offers no Mac version. On a Mac the Ear series plays audio, but ANC and EQ stay on your phone.

Can I change ANC on CMF Buds Pro 2 from a Mac?

Not in this build: CMF models aren’t matched and Mac control is planned. The experimental sound handoff shows up in the menu but hasn’t been tried on CMF; macOS shows battery if the earbuds report it.

Why does the battery sometimes not show on the Mac?

Nothing battery is read after the activation handshake. If the handshake gets no answer, only the battery macOS reports is left.

What if the EQ on my Ear (a) doesn’t change?

It is experimental and we haven’t had an Ear (a) on our desk. Send what you tried and what happened with the report link below; the status changes on that basis.

Does the number of reports change the status label?

No. The verification dashboard shows the number of successful TestFlight reports next to each model’s label. The count never replaces the label, and no fixed number turns a model into “verified”.
