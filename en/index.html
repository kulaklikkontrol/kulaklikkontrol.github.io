How it works

Sony headphones accept settings over a Bluetooth RFCOMM channel using a small framed protocol (start/end byte, checksum, an ack for every message). The app opens that channel with IOBluetooth and talks to the headphones directly. No Sony account, no network access, sandboxed, no analytics or tracking. Written in Swift and SwiftUI.

Control your Sony WH-1000XM5 from a Mac

Is there a Sony Headphones Connect app for Mac?

Headphone Control is an independent Mac app for changing headphone settings from the menu bar. Only WH-1000XM5 has been tested on real hardware; WH-1000XM4, WH-1000XM6 and other models are untested.

How do I turn noise canceling on or off for Sony WH-1000XM5 on a Mac?

Pair the headphones in System Settings → Bluetooth, open Headphone Control and click Noise Canceling, Ambient Sound or Off in the menu bar panel. You can also use the ⌃⌥⌘N keyboard shortcut.

Can I change the Sony WH-1000XM5 equalizer from a Mac?

Yes. Headphone Control shows every equalizer preset the headphones offer, plus Clear Bass and the custom bands. Changes are sent to the headphones instantly.

Frequently asked questions

How do I join the beta?

  1. Get Apple's free TestFlight app from the Mac App Store.
  2. Open the beta link and click “Accept” → “Install”.
  3. Connect your headphones and try the mode, equalizer and battery reading. If something doesn't work, use “Send Beta Feedback” in TestFlight and include your headphone model and macOS version.

I accepted, but the app didn't install

“Accept” only accepts the invitation. Open the TestFlight app, pick Headphone Control and click Install. If there is no Install button, your Mac may not be on macOS 14 or later: Apple's generic page says macOS 13, but this app needs macOS 14+. If it still fails, email us.

Which headphones are supported?

Real-hardware tested: WH-1000XM5 only. User-reported working (not tested by the developer): WH-CH720N. Implemented but untested: WH-1000XM4, WH-1000XM6, WF-1000XM4, WF-1000XM5, WF-1000XM6, LinkBuds S, LinkBuds Fit, LinkBuds Open and ULT WEAR. The app only shows the settings your headphones report.

The app can't find my headphones.

  • Pair and connect the headphones in System Settings → Bluetooth first.
  • Allow Bluetooth access when macOS asks. If you denied it: System Settings → Privacy & Security → Bluetooth.
  • Close the headphones' own app on your phone; only one app can control the headphones at a time.
  • Click “Scan again” in the app.

I don't have the headphones yet. Can I try the app?

Yes. On the “Not connected” screen, click “Try demo mode” to explore the whole interface with sample data.

What is the keyboard shortcut?

By default ⌃⌥⌘N cycles Noise Canceling → Ambient Sound → Off. You can change it in the Settings tab, and add shortcuts for next equalizer preset, next profile, Speak-to-Chat and “Play on Mac”.

How do profiles and automatic rules work?

In the Profiles tab, save mode, equalizer and Speak-to-Chat as a profile. Add rules to apply a profile automatically during a call, while an app you choose is open, or while the screen is locked; your previous settings return when the rule ends.

Widgets, Control Center and Siri

Right-click the desktop, choose “Edit Widgets” and add the Headphone Control widget. On macOS 26 you can add “Noise mode”, “Noise canceling” and “Play on Mac” buttons to Control Center. The app's actions are ready in the Shortcuts app, and you can ask Siri “Headphone Control battery”. Widgets and buttons need the app running (staying in the menu bar is enough).

Do you collect my data?

No. The app never connects to the internet and collects no data. See the privacy policy.
