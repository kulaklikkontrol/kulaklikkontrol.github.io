SHORT DESCRIPTION
Headphone Control puts noise canceling, ambient sound, EQ and multipoint settings for Sony headphones in your Mac's menu bar.

DETAILS
Change everyday headphone settings without reaching for a phone. The app also includes profiles, automatic rules during calls or while a selected app is open, widgets, Shortcuts actions and keyboard shortcuts. It sends settings directly over Bluetooth, requires no account and collects no analytics.

HARDWARE TESTING
Only WH-1000XM5 has been tested on real hardware. WH-1000XM4, WH-1000XM6, WF-1000XM4/XM5/XM6, LinkBuds S/Fit/Open, WH-CH720N and ULT WEAR are implemented from the protocol but still need real hardware testing. Settings vary by model. Please do not describe these other models as hardware-verified.
