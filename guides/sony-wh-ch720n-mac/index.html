Pair the WH-CH720N with your Mac

Sony documents Mac pairing for this model on macOS 11 or later. The headphones remember up to 8 devices.

  1. Put the headphones in pairing modeWith the headphones off, press and hold the power button for about 5 seconds or more. The blue indicator flashes twice in a row, repeatedly, and you hear “Pairing”.
  2. Connect from System SettingsOn the Mac, open System Settings > Bluetooth and click Connect next to WH-CH720N. If a passkey is requested, Sony’s guide says to enter 0000. Pairing mode ends after about 5 minutes.
  3. Choose the headphones as outputPick WH-CH720N in the Sound menu or System Settings > Sound > Output and check playback. Later, to reconnect, Control-click WH-CH720N in Bluetooth settings and choose Connect.

What the user report does and doesn’t prove

On 17 November 2026, one beta tester publicly reported a positive result with their WH-CH720N. The developer does not own this model, so it has not been tested by us. One report covers one firmware, one Mac and the features that person tried.

That is why the site says “user-reported” rather than “tested”. Reports that name the controls tested help us assess this experimental model. If you need a controller verified on CH720N hardware today, SonyBridge’s README describes the WH-CH720N as its fully hardware-verified model.

Questions

Does Headphone Control work with Sony WH-CH720N?

The WH-CH720N is experimental. One beta user reported a positive result on their own setup. The developer has yet to test this model.

How do I pair WH-CH720N with a Mac?

Hold the power button for about 5 seconds until the indicator flashes blue twice and you hear “Pairing”. Then click Connect next to WH-CH720N in System Settings > Bluetooth and enter 0000 if asked.

Is there an official Sony app for WH-CH720N on Mac?

No. Sony’s Sound Connect app is for iPhone and Android. Use it for firmware updates; a Mac controller only changes everyday settings.
