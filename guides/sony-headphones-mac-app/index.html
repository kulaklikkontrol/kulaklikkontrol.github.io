A Sony headphones app for your Mac: AirPods-style switching

Sony’s own app runs on phones. On a Mac you get a Bluetooth menu and not much else. Headphone Control puts audio switching, noise canceling and EQ for Sony headphones in the menu bar. It is free on the Mac App Store.

What “AirPods-style switching” means here

AirPods follow you between your Apple devices. Sony headphones can stay connected to a Mac and an iPhone at the same time (multipoint), but the sound stays wherever it started. Getting it back usually means pausing the phone or digging through Bluetooth menus.

Headphone Control shows both devices in the menu bar. Pick your Mac under Audio source (or click Play here) and the headphones move the audio to your Mac. Pick the iPhone and it goes back. No re-pairing, no Bluetooth settings.

What you get in 1.0 (Mac App Store)

  • One-click audio source: choose Mac or iPhone from the menu bar.
  • The headphones’ own auto-switch setting: turn on the Sony feature that moves audio to whichever device starts playing. The headphones make that decision; the app just turns it on or off.
  • Noise canceling, ambient sound and off, ambient level and focus on voice.
  • EQ presets, Clear Bass and the band sliders.
  • Battery in the menu bar, with low-battery alerts at 20, 10 and 5%.
  • A global shortcut to cycle noise modes.

What 1.3 adds (beta)

Version 1.3 is in App Store review and available now on TestFlight. The headline addition is optional automatic switching from the Mac side: when audio starts on your Mac, the app can ask the headphones to move the sound to the Mac. It is off by default and waits 15 seconds between attempts so it doesn’t fight your phone.

1.3 also adds profiles and rules, a “Play on Mac” keyboard shortcut, battery history, widgets and eight languages. A Mac-to-Mac handoff option exists for some non-Sony headphones; it is experimental and has not been tested on hardware.

Honest limits

  • Hardware-tested on the WH-1000XM5 only. WH-1000XM4, XM6, WF earbuds and LinkBuds are experimental.
  • It does not update headphone firmware. Keep Sony’s phone app for that.
  • Automatic switching in 1.3 is a best-effort helper, not Apple’s AirPods system. If the phone is actively playing, it can still win.
  • Independent software. Sony doesn’t make or endorse it.

Set it up in two minutes

  1. Pair the headphones with your Mac in System Settings → Bluetooth, and keep them paired with your iPhone.
  2. Install Headphone Control from the Mac App Store and allow Bluetooth access when asked.
  3. Open the menu bar icon. Your Mac and iPhone appear under Audio source; click one to move the sound.
  4. Want automatic switching from the Mac side? Join the 1.3 beta and turn on “Switch to Mac when audio starts on it”.

Questions

Is there an official Sony Headphones Connect app for Mac?
No. Sony’s app (now called Sound Connect) is for iPhone and Android. On a Mac, macOS can pair and play through Sony headphones, but it doesn’t expose Sony’s noise-canceling, EQ or source controls.
Is Headphone Control free?
Yes. Version 1.0 is free on the Mac App Store and the 1.3 beta is free on TestFlight. No account, no tracking.
Does it switch automatically like AirPods?
In 1.0 you switch with one click, or turn on the headphones’ own auto-switch setting. 1.3 (beta) adds an optional Mac-side automatic switch when Mac audio starts. It is off by default.
Which Sony headphones work?
Only the WH-1000XM5 has been tested on real hardware. Other models may work and are marked experimental.
Which macOS version do I need?
macOS 14 Sonoma or later.
