Change ANC and EQ step by step

  1. Connect and installPair WH-1000XM5 in System Settings > Bluetooth. On macOS 14+, install Apple TestFlight, accept the beta invitation, then click Install. Open Headphone Control and allow Bluetooth access.
  2. Change noise controlOpen the menu-bar panel and wait for Connected. Choose Noise Canceling for isolation, Ambient Sound to hear around you, or Off to disable those modes. With Ambient selected, adjust its level gradually.
  3. Compare EQ at the same volumePlay a familiar track at a comfortable level. Choose an EQ preset, then compare it with Off at the same playback volume. Adjust Clear Bass or the custom bands a little at a time.
  4. Check the resultListen for the change and check the selected setting in the panel. Test noise control and EQ separately. If the interface changes but the sound does not, report that exact behavior rather than counting it as a successful control.

What this changes, and what it does not

ANC and Ambient Sound are headphone modes. They are different from your Mac’s output volume and from a music app’s software EQ. A browser or music-player equalizer changes that app’s audio; it is not a Sony headphone setting.

Headphone Control is not a firmware updater. Keep Sony Sound Connect for updates and model-specific setup. The free beta is closed source and requires macOS 14+.

If the control does not take effect

No sound at all
Check System Settings > Sound > Output and play audio before troubleshooting the controller.
Audio works, panel does not connect
Check the app’s Bluetooth permission. As a diagnostic, close the phone controller and other desktop controllers, reconnect the headphones and retry one app at a time.
Music changes quality during a call
Apple explains that using the headset microphone can switch Bluetooth into a lower-quality two-way mode. This is separate from EQ. Close apps using that microphone and reselect the headphones if needed.

Questions

Can the macOS Bluetooth menu change Sony EQ?

The built-in Mac Bluetooth and Sound settings handle pairing and audio routing. They do not provide Sony’s custom headphone EQ controls. Use Sony’s mobile app or an independent compatible controller.

Will these steps work on XM4, XM6 or WF-XM5?

Only WH-1000XM5 has been hardware-tested with Headphone Control. WH-1000XM4, WH-1000XM6 and WF-1000XM5 are untested; their model pages explain how to report a beta test.
