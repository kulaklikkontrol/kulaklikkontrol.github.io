How to control Sony LinkBuds on Mac: pairing, ANC & EQ

“LinkBuds” covers several Sony earbuds: the original ring-shaped LinkBuds, LinkBuds S, Fit and Open. They pair differently, and none has been tested with Headphone Control yet.

Pair LinkBuds with your Mac

Sony documents Mac pairing for LinkBuds and LinkBuds S on macOS 10.15 or later. LinkBuds Fit and LinkBuds Open have their own Sony help guides; follow the one for your model if the button or light differs.

  1. Original LinkBuds: use the case buttonPut both earbuds in the case, close the lid, then open it again. Press and hold the pairing button on the case for about 5 seconds until the indicator starts flashing blue.
  2. LinkBuds S: keep the lid openWith both earbuds in the case and the lid open, hold the pairing button on the back of the case for about 5 seconds. Sony notes it won’t enter pairing mode with the lid closed.
  3. Connect and choose the outputOn the Mac, open System Settings > Bluetooth and click Connect next to your LinkBuds, entering 0000 if a passkey is requested. Then pick them in System Settings > Sound > Output.

Which LinkBuds do you have?

The original LinkBuds (WF-L900) have an open ring design and no noise canceling. LinkBuds S, Fit and Open are separate products with their own firmware and feature sets. A result on one LinkBuds model does not carry over to another.

Headphone Control has not been tested on any LinkBuds model. When you report, the exact name on the box or in Bluetooth settings matters most.

Questions

Can I control Sony LinkBuds from a Mac?

Sony has no Mac app. Headphone Control is a free macOS 14+ beta that implements the Sony protocol, but no LinkBuds model has been tested. Report your exact model and result in GitHub Discussion #2.

How do I put LinkBuds S in pairing mode for a Mac?

Put both earbuds in the case with the lid open and hold the pairing button on the back of the case for about 5 seconds. Then click Connect in System Settings > Bluetooth.

Do the original LinkBuds have noise canceling?

No. The original ring-shaped LinkBuds (WF-L900) are an open design without noise canceling. LinkBuds S has noise canceling.
