How to read this comparison

This page is published by the Headphone Control developer. Facts were checked against the linked official pages on 16 November 2026. We have not hands-on tested SonyBridge or Open Sound Connect. Their documented features are not our hardware verification.

Which should you choose?

  • Just need music on your Mac? Start with built-in Bluetooth and Sound settings.
  • Need firmware updates? Use Sony Sound Connect on your phone.
  • Want a free XM5 menu-bar beta on macOS 14+? Try Headphone Control, knowing it is closed source.
  • Want source code or an older Mac option? Check SonyBridge’s macOS 12+ build and its own model-testing notes.
  • Want documented scenes and scheduled settings? Compare Open Sound Connect’s paid macOS 15+ app and its current model list.

No desktop controller here should be assumed to replace every official Sony feature. For other Headphone Control models, a successful connection is only the beginning of a test.

Questions

Is this an independent review?

No. It is a sourced comparison published by the Headphone Control developer. We disclose that relationship and have not tested the other Mac apps ourselves.

Which option is open source?

SonyBridge and Open Sound Connect publish MIT-licensed source. Headphone Control is closed source. Check each official project for current builds, terms and model coverage.
