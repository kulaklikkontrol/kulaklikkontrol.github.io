Pair for Mac audio

  1. Enter pairing modeFor a second device, hold the power button for about 7 seconds until the blue indicator repeatedly flashes twice and you hear the pairing prompt.
  2. Choose the full headphone nameOpen System Settings > Bluetooth. Select WH-1000XM4, not LE_WH-1000XM4. Sony says the full name can take 30–60 seconds to appear.
  3. Check Mac audioChoose WH-1000XM4 in System Settings > Sound > Output and play a familiar track at a comfortable volume.

XM4 multipoint: two connections, one playback stream

Sony’s XM4 guide says to enable “Connect to 2 devices simultaneously” in its mobile app. Pair each device, connect both, then stop playback on the first before starting the second. Two connections do not mean two mixed audio streams.

The XM4 guide also says multipoint disables LDAC and uses AAC or SBC. This is a headphone setting, not a feature added by a Mac controller. If the Mac is silent, pause the phone before changing anything else.

Help test this model

Keep the Mac connected and test connection status before changing settings. Try one ANC change, one EQ preset and the battery reading separately. If EQ does not respond, report it separately from ANC; a partial result is useful.

  1. Connection: does the app show the right model and Connected?
  2. Noise control: try ANC and Ambient. Did the headphones actually change mode?
  3. EQ: compare one preset with Off at the same volume. Did the sound change?
  4. Battery and device switching: note the values shown. If you try switching between Mac and phone, record the result separately.

Questions

Does Headphone Control work with WH-1000XM4?

WH-1000XM4 is untested in Headphone Control. We cannot promise ANC, EQ, battery or multipoint controls on it. Only WH-1000XM5 has been tested on real hardware.

Will Bluetooth audio work without Headphone Control?

Sony documents Mac pairing for this model. Normal audio pairing is independent of Headphone Control and does not prove that its ANC or EQ controls work.
