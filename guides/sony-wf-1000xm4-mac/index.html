How to control Sony WF-1000XM4 on Mac: pairing, ANC & EQ

The WF-1000XM4 pairs with a Mac like any Bluetooth earbuds. Changing noise canceling or EQ from the Mac needs a controller app, and on this model ours has not been tested yet.

Pair the WF-1000XM4 with your Mac

Sony documents Mac pairing for this model on macOS 10.14 or later. These steps get you audio. They say nothing about whether a third-party app can change the earbuds’ settings.

  1. Put the earbuds in pairing modeThe very first time, take both earbuds out of the case: they enter pairing mode automatically. To add the Mac as another device, wear both earbuds and hold your fingers on both touch sensors for about 5 seconds until you hear “Bluetooth pairing”.
  2. Connect from System SettingsOn the Mac, open System Settings > Bluetooth and click Connect next to WF-1000XM4. If a passkey is requested, Sony’s guide says to enter 0000. Pairing mode ends after about 5 minutes.
  3. Choose the earbuds as outputPick WF-1000XM4 from the Sound menu in the menu bar or in System Settings > Sound > Output, then play something before you open a controller.

WF-1000XM4 is not the WF-1000XM5

Headphone Control was built and tested on the WH-1000XM5 over-ear headset. The WF-1000XM4 is an earlier earbud model with its own firmware, case battery and touch controls. We have no hardware result for it yet, so we can’t say which controls work.

Once connected, Sony’s guide says the earbuds reconnect when you take them out of the case. If they don’t, choose WF-1000XM4 in Bluetooth settings again. The earbuds remember up to 8 devices.

Own a WF-1000XM4? Run the 2-minute test

Wear both earbuds and confirm Mac audio first. Then check ANC, ambient sound and EQ one at a time, and note which battery values appear: left, right, case or a single number. If something is missing, say so. A “didn’t connect” report is still useful. Post it in Discussion #2 or use the options below.

  1. Connection: does the app show the right model and Connected?
  2. Noise control: try ANC and Ambient. Did the headphones actually change mode?
  3. EQ: compare one preset with Off at the same volume. Did the sound change?
  4. Battery and device switching: note the values shown. If you try switching between Mac and phone, record the result separately.

On your Mac: install Apple TestFlight, open the invitation, then Accept → Install. Accepting alone does not install the app. Requires macOS 14+, even if Apple’s generic invitation text says 13. App Store 1.0 is under review.

Questions

Can I control Sony WF-1000XM4 ANC from a Mac?

Sony has no Mac app. Headphone Control is a free macOS 14+ beta that implements the Sony protocol, but it has not been tested on WF-1000XM4 yet. Try it and report the result in GitHub Discussion #2.

How do I pair WF-1000XM4 with a Mac?

Wear both earbuds and hold both touch sensors for about 5 seconds until you hear “Bluetooth pairing”. Then click Connect next to WF-1000XM4 in System Settings > Bluetooth. Enter 0000 if a passkey is requested.

Does the WH-1000XM5 test cover the WF-1000XM4?

No. They are different products with different firmware. Only the WH-1000XM5 has been tested by the developer.
