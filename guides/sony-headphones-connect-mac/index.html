Headphones Connect is now Sound Connect

Sony renamed its mobile app to Sony | Sound Connect in October 2024. The official Apple listing is for iPhone; it is not a native macOS controller. It offers EQ, noise control and headphone software updates. Keep it for firmware and Sony-specific setup.

Connecting headphones to a Mac for music is a separate task. macOS can pair them and select them as audio output without a Sony app. That does not give the Bluetooth menu Sony’s full ANC and EQ controls.

An independent Mac option: Headphone Control

Headphone Control puts noise canceling, ambient sound, EQ and device switching in the menu bar. It is a free, closed-source beta for macOS 14+. Only the WH-1000XM5 has been tested on real hardware. WH-1000XM4, WH-1000XM6 and WF-1000XM5 are untested.

The screenshot below is from a real WH-1000XM5. It is evidence for that model only. This app is independently developed and is not made or endorsed by Sony.

Choose by the job you need done

Pair headphones or change audio output
Start with macOS Bluetooth and Sound settings. You do not need another app for ordinary listening.
Update headphone firmware
Use Sony’s official mobile app. Headphone Control is not a firmware updater.
Adjust ANC or EQ while working on a Mac
Try a desktop controller. Check its own model list and minimum macOS version first.
Read or modify the source
SonyBridge and Open Sound Connect publish their source. Headphone Control does not.

Install the beta on a Mac

  1. Check that your Mac runs macOS 14 or later.
  2. Install Apple’s TestFlight app on the Mac, then open the beta invitation.
  3. Choose Accept, then Install inside TestFlight. Accepting the invitation alone does not install the app.
  4. Pair the headphones in macOS Bluetooth settings, open Headphone Control and allow Bluetooth access when requested.

Apple’s invitation page may show a generic macOS 13 requirement. The app itself requires macOS 14+. App Store 1.0 is under review; the available download here is the TestFlight beta.

Questions

Is there an official Sony Headphones Connect app for Mac?

The official Apple listing for Sony Sound Connect, formerly Headphones Connect, is for iPhone. Independent Mac controllers are separate products, not Sony apps.

Does Headphone Control replace all of Sony’s mobile app?

No. Use Sony’s mobile app for firmware updates and Sony-specific setup. Headphone Control focuses on daily controls on a Mac.

Is Headphone Control open source?

No. It is free and closed source. SonyBridge and Open Sound Connect are source-available under the MIT license in their official repositories.
